Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use SuGar:

  • Account Information: Name, email, age, gender, profile photos, bio
  • Location Data: Approximate location to show nearby users
  • Messages: Text, images, and audio shared in chats
  • Device Info: FCM token for push notifications

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the app: show profiles, enable chatting, send notifications, and verify users. We do not sell your data.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely on Firebase (Google Cloud servers). Media files are stored in Firebase Storage.

4. Data Deletion

You can request account deletion anytime from the app's Profile → Delete My Account & Data. We will delete all your data within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob for ads. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs. We use Firebase Authentication for login.
