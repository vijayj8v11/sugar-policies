# Privacy Policy — SuGar

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SuGar ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects the personal information of users ("you") of the SuGar mobile application ("the App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Profile information:** name, age, gender, role/preference, bio, interests, and photographs (including profile pictures, story media, and selfie/document images submitted for verification).
- **Account information:** your email address and Google account identifiers when you sign in with Google.

### 1.2 Information We Collect Automatically
- **Location information:** your approximate GPS location, used to show you people nearby. We store latitude/longitude co-ordinates in our database so other users can see the approximate distance to you.
- **Usage data:** interactions with features such as swipes, matches, messages, and story views.
- **Device information:** basic device and OS information needed for push notifications and ad delivery.

### 1.3 Information From Other Users
- Messages, reactions, and content sent to you by other users are stored so they can be displayed in your chat history.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the App's core features (profiles, swiping, matching, chat, stories, and verification).
- Facilitate matches and conversations between users.
- Verify user identity through our optional live face scan and photo/document verification features.
- Send push notifications about matches, messages, and app updates.
- Serve advertising through Google AdMob and measure ad performance.
- Improve the App, detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents, and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How We Share Your Information
- **With other users:** your public profile information (photos, name, age, bio, interests, and distance) is visible to other users. Your messages and stories are visible to the recipients you share them with.
- **With service providers:** we share data with trusted third parties who help us operate the App, including:
  - **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging) — stores your account, profile, messages, photos, and verification media.
  - **Google AdMob** — serves advertisements and may collect advertising identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) subject to its own privacy policy.
  - **Google Play services and ML Kit** — used for sign-in, location, and face detection during verification.
- **Legal and safety:** we may disclose information when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the public.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 4. Verification Data
When you choose to complete Live Verification or Document Verification:
- We upload your selfie and government-issued identification image to our secure cloud storage.
- Face data is processed locally on your device for liveness detection and is used only to confirm your identity.
- Verification images are stored securely and are only accessible by you. Verified status (a badge) is displayed to other users, but your actual identification document is never made public.

## 5. Data Security
We use industry-standard safeguards, including secure HTTPS transmission and Firebase's built-in security, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8). Some data may be retained longer where required by law.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices
You may:
- **Access and update** your profile information at any time in the App.
- **Opt out** of advertising personalization through your device settings.
- **Delete** individual messages or your entire account.
- **Request** that we delete your personal data by contacting us at the address below.

To delete your account and data, contact us and we will remove your profile, messages, photos, and verification data from our servers.

## 9. Advertising
The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data (including advertising identifiers and approximate location) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalization in your device settings.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you in the App. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
